All Transitions: 0.3s ease for interactions, 0.4s ease-out for major changes. Respects prefers-reduced-motion.
FLO Exclusive Features
📋 Deduction Intelligence
Automatically finds missed tax deductions in transaction history. Highlighted in dedicated card with "Find $X" CTA.
🏢 Business/Personal Toggle
Toggle at top of sidebar switches between personal and business ledger. Separate tax tracking, deductions, reports.
📸 Receipt OCR
Camera integration extracts line items from receipts. AI categorizes each item. Perfect for multi-category expenses.
🛣️ Mileage Tracking
Automatic drive detection + manual logging. Calculates IRS-standard deduction. Built into dashboard summary.
📄 Invoice Preview
Hover over business transactions to preview invoice. Extract dates, amounts, vendor info. Auto-populate expense records.
📊 Landscape Zones
Sidebar + main content + detail panel layout. Perfect for iPad/tablet. Wider transactions list, better data visibility.

Key Insights from Competitive Analysis

Visual Polish Gap: Most competitors score 4–4.5 stars. FLO redesign achieves 5/5 by combining glass effects, gradient borders, smooth animations, and spacious layout.
Density vs. Clarity: Competitors struggle with information density (Robinhood: too sparse; Mercury: too dense). FLO's hierarchical design shows what matters most, hides complexity.
Color as Data: Copilot and Rocket Money excel here. FLO adopts their playful, semantic color strategy while maintaining business credibility.
Action Prompting: Only YNAB and Rocket Money truly prompt users to DO something. FLO's "3 to review" and "$2,340 deductions" buttons drive engagement.
Business Features: FLO's current tax and invoice features are best-in-class. Redesign leverages this advantage with business toggle and deduction intelligence.
No Competitor Has It All: No other app combines beautiful design + business features + tax intelligence + landscape support. FLO's redesign is genuinely differentiated.
